About

I'm a clinical dietitian trained at Sri Ramachandra Institute of Higher Education and Research. I bring hands-on clinical experience at Apollo Cancer Center, where I provided specialised Medical Nutrition Therapy and dietary counselling for oncology patients, and managed enteral and parenteral nutrition plans based on individual needs, treatment protocols and side effects. Alongside hospital practice, I run a diabetes-focused OPD consultation and a nutrition education page for people who want the reasoning behind their diet, not just a chart to follow.

Areas of practice: Oncology, renal, gastric, pregnancy and lactation, paediatric nutrition, Diabetology and cardio.
